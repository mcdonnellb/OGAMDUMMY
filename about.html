About OGAM

From a Korean home kitchen to modern tables around the world.

Meet Jay Choi — Founder of OGAM

Jay grew up surrounded by the flavours of her mother’s kitchen: slow-cooked stews, bold spices, and the warmth of shared meals. Food was never just food — it was how stories were told, comfort was offered, and family stayed connected.

After moving to the UK, Jay began recreating those dishes for friends and family. What started as weekend dinners quickly became a word-of-mouth favourite. People kept asking the same question: “Can we take this home?”

Their encouragement inspired her to bottle the flavours she loved. OGAM was born from that desire to share the taste of home — in a way that fits busy, modern lives.

The OGAM Story

OGAM is built on a simple idea: authentic Korean flavours should be easy to enjoy, wherever you are. Every bottle is crafted to capture the depth, balance, and warmth of Korean home cooking — without the need for long ingredient lists or complex techniques.

The sauces are designed to be versatile: pour over grilled meats, toss through vegetables, glaze roasted dishes, or use as a dipping sauce. Whether it’s a quick midweek meal or a table full of shared plates, OGAM turns everyday cooking into something a little more special.

From the first recipe tests in Jay’s kitchen to the bottles now on shelves, the focus has stayed the same: real flavour, honest ingredients, and food that brings people together.

OGAM is more than a sauce brand — it’s a bridge between heritage and modern cooking, between Korean tradition and global kitchens.
