About OGAM

From a Korean home kitchen to your table

Meet Jay Choi Founder of OGAM

Twenty-five years ago, I boarded a plane to London for the first time as a student hoping to learn English. Life eventually brought me to Ireland, where I met my Irish husband, built a family and worked as a Chartered Management accountant while raising our children. Ten years ago, our family moved to Cork following our son’s autism diagnosis. That journey completely changed the way I looked at food, health and everyday life. Wanting to better understand and support my son, I began learning more about the connection between the brain, gut health and nutrition. Over time, our kitchen and lifestyle changed too.

I became far more mindful about the ingredients we used and started preparing more food from scratch at home. Through that process, I discovered the real importance of good food — not only for nourishment, but for comfort, connection and wellbeing. What started as a personal journey for my family eventually became OGAM. As a Korean founder living in Ireland, I wanted to create sauces that bring authentic Korean flavours into Irish homes using clean and simple ingredients. Every jar is inspired by the same care and attention I give to the food I prepare for my own family.

Today, OGAM continues to grow from Cork, sharing Korean-inspired flavours with people across Ireland. My hope is that everyone who enjoys OGAM experiences not only great flavour, but also warmth, joy and connection through food. Thank you for being part of our journey.
