Meet Kemi — Founder of OGAM

Kemi grew up surrounded by the flavours of her mother’s kitchen: slow‑cooked stews, bold spices, and the warmth of shared meals. After moving to the UK, she began recreating those dishes for friends and family. Their encouragement inspired her to bottle the flavours she loved.

OGAM is her way of sharing heritage, culture, and comfort through food — bringing authentic African flavours to modern kitchens.

The OGAM Story

OGAM began as a passion project rooted in tradition. Every sauce is crafted from recipes passed down through generations, using real ingredients and bold flavours. The brand celebrates African culture, community, and the joy of shared meals.
