OGAM: Crafted for the Five Senses

Founded by Jay Choi, OGAM preserves the bold, balanced flavours of the Korea she grew up in — handmade in small batches here in Ireland. After 24 years abroad, a move to Cork and a new chapter of motherhood inspired Jay explore holisitc health and wellness through food. Her award‑winning sauces are crafted with minimal, honest ingredients and the intuition of someone who cooks by memory, not measurement.

OGAM means “five senses”. Jay’s goal is simple: to let people experience Korean food the way it’s meant to be with all five senses.
