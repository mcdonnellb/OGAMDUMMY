Meet Kemi — Founder of OGAM

Kemi grew up surrounded by the flavours of her mother’s kitchen: slow‑cooked stews, bold spices, and the warmth of shared meals. After moving to the UK, she began recreating those dishes for friends and family. OGAM is her way of sharing heritage, culture, and comfort through food.
